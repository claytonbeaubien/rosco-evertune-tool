Where the numbers come from
ET published by EverTune, RG Rosco bench rule, not manufacturer guidance.
  • ET Body + string height must exceed 53 mm (2.1"). Under 13 mm string height is F/T, over is G. Bridge Buying Guide.
  • ET Saddle height windows from the body top, E strings: F/T 8.9 to 13.5 mm, G 14.5 to 19.2 mm. ET001 F&T and G Overall Dimensions, 2017e01. Other strings can go higher on the 400 mm radius.
  • ET F/T minimum body 44.45 mm (1 3/4"); under that the footplate mounts on a custom back plate riser. F and F7 Model pages.
  • RG Top riser shifts the whole F window up by its height, and adds to every backside depth. Max 6 mm measured at the scale line; above that the bridge fouls the bridge pickup, and F + 6 is the G window anyway. 3-sided, front open (Zendesk 22417).
  • RG Back of the body: route the footplate's own thickness (2 mm assumed, confirm) so it sits flush. The footplate may sit up to 1 mm off its design position, so the recess is pulled back toward design only when it has to be. Whatever stands proud of the back face is the back cover riser, rounded up to 0.5. If even zero recess leaves the footplate over 1 mm high, shim it back to design and the riser grows by the shim. The riser counts in the candidacy stack. Over 6 mm gets bulky and needs client approval.
  • ET T, G and G Long are 6-string only; 7 and 8 strings are F7 and F8. G standard is made to come right to the bridge pickup bezel of a standard Les Paul: full scale length point to the back edge of the bezel 0.55" (14 mm) or less is G standard, longer is G Long, whose plate stretches to the pickup so there is no gap. EverTune, G model long versus G model standard.
  • RG Bridge choice on a 6: F up to a 4.5 mm top riser, G above that. 7 and 8 strings go F7 / F8 with a riser up to 6 mm. T and G take no top riser.
  • ET Saddle pitch 10.4 mm on average, saddles float a little; E to e works out about 52 / 62.4 / 72.8 for 6 / 7 / 8. RG Old bridge narrower than the EverTune (outer strings move out toward the fretboard edges): 2.5 mm total is fine, 2.5 to 4 check the edges, over 4 flagged. Old bridge wider (strings move in, e.g. a vintage Strat at 56.4): 4.5 is fine, 4.5 to 6 check pole alignment, over 6 flagged.
  • RG Measure string height with one reference string set to a known 12th-fret action, before removing the old bridge. The EverTune saddle at mid-window should reproduce that height.
